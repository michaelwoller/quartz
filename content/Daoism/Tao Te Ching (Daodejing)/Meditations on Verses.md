## 1

The Dao cannot be directly known or understood, which makes it enternal. Being able to name anything gives it creation as it is a step towards understanding. The things in which you wish to know, and name, are not knowable and nameable as much as you will.

If you do not wish to name and understand the Dao in this way, you can start to see the mystery. The desire to understand these unknowable things only leads to understanding your own predilections for what they are. 

Regardless they all come from "darkness within darkness", which is the gateway to understanding. To admit to not knowing about not knowing is how you come to understand.

The name "The Dao" only represents a piece of what is unknowable. We must come to understand that we cannot *know* The Dao as we label it. That is the first step of understanding its nature

## 2

Concepts are not definitive. Subjectivity exists as a quality. Beauty can be ugly. Good can be bad. Being cannot exist without non-being. Justice cannot exist without sin. Difficult and easy. Long and short. High and low. Before and after. 

Concepts exist in dualities or more. They need representation and relativity to exist. 

The individual we should strive to be exists within these dualities. They act without trying to act. They teach without speaking. They let things come and go.

They do not wish to possess or have expectations. When they finish work, they forget the work. 

This gives each aspect of their being eternity, as it exists between the duality of concepts. Their behavior cannot be known if it does not take a discrete form. At this point they start to mimic The Dao. 

Most likely, this is done through extreme amounts of mindfulness in all waking behavior. Mindful actions and meditation are both difficult and easy, paradoxically. To "just exist" is difficult for us, but yet it is not a difficult action to do.

## 3

Do not overvalue things, this leads to disorder. Praise leads to ego and the stripping of others' power.  People steal and covet when you put things on a pedestal.  

To value one act and object above another gives way to a dilemma in this way, both individually and collectively. I am unconvinced that being confident or self-assured prevents these actions. One will always covet more for whatever excuses, happiness, security, etc. To let go is extreme, but is the only way out.

The master (who we should strive to emulate) will empty minds and weaken ambition. This toughens resolve. The master destroys what they know and desire. This creates confusion for those who think they know and have accepted desire.

This is why people are so apprehensive towards these Daoist concepts. It is easy to make excuses and justify the state of being one has been raised in and has existed within for their entire lives. To even question these things of basic human nature is blasphemous, yet most don't realize their innate defensiveness to this kind of dilemma. 

Practice not doing and not wanting. This will lead towards a path you were not aware existed and a state of being you did not realize was possible. 

## 4

These are descriptions of the duality The Dao exists between. It does not take a discrete named aspect as it exists in a weird flux of being. 

It is a well that is used but is never used.
It is an empty void what is infinitely filled up.
It is hidden form view but is omni present.
It is older than God.

Essentially, it seems to be the cosmic fabric of reality, that not even God created. Before their was the universe, there was God. But how could God exist in an existenceless space. The Dao might explain this. It is the Dao that surrounds us all yet we never feel it. 

It is a reality in nature despite the fact that it is inherently non-reality. It seems like this idea is relatively intuitive to the religious and atheist. Physics can help us explain much, but there are limitations in our understanding, potentially forever. What is infinitely small? What is infinitely big? What came before time? Is it even possible to exist *before* time exists? How did the big bang happen before time existed. How did the big bang come about from 0-dimensional space? There is more than we comprehend, and it is everywhere. This is where The Dao exists. Potentially, this is The Dao itself.

## 5

More meditations on the nature of The Dao. It does not take sides as it gave birth to both good and evil. It exists in a spectrum of neutrality. Therefore, the master who emulated the Dao does not take sides. They welcome both saints and sinners as learners that can seek to let go. If anything, this would seem to equalize both in a way that somewhat conflicts with Christianity. Some sects of Christianity believe in redemption of souls (at least the majority of souls) that reside in hell. But Christians believe we all fall short of heaven, and don't deserve to go there if it were not for the atonement of Christ. 

The Dao takes no sides as it exists beyond good and evil. One cannot be looked at with judgement from a thing that cannot judge. Good cannot exist without evil, and vice-versa. How can one condemn the evil if they are necessary for us to be considered good? 

Though Daoism is not morally agnostic. There are morals within it, but those are described later.

The Dao is like a bellows (metaphor)
It is empty, but filled with indefinite use
The more you use it, the more it produces
The more you talk about it the *less* you understand

One cannot come to easily think they understand the Dao. It's existence seems intuitive and yet overly complex. 

We are asked to "hold on to the center" of understanding and nonunderstanding

This is the middle way

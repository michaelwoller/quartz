I feel ahedoniac and forfreonted with the same emotions I’ have felt since I was 9. I wonder if theya re different or more matured due to age, but most likely not. I have seen depression, anxiety, and the likes of all neurotic experiences persist well into adulthood, far past the points I have even been, and where, I presume, I will eventually travel. I have to wonder, as I always have to wonder and will always wonder like I always HAVE had wondered in the past, what the purpose of it is.

  

I don’t mean the teleological purpose or some kind of cosmological purpose of these kidneys of emotions from God or whatnot. I mean when I sit with my experiences, trying to understand what the source of my emotions are and what they can say about me, what they say about my experience in the moment, how that reflects on my values and desires in the moment, and how I should consider these emotions, I want to understand if there is important weight to them or if I should just let them go.

  

That is, is this something to be gripped about or let flow away in an act of mindfulness. It’s very sad that every strong sad experience I feel is something I have to question the merits of. I suppose its better than presuming it either all is too important and signals to much about me and life, or that it signals nothing and my feelings are all meaningless. It still takes constant waking effort to make an attempt, and the attempt is likely never going to come to complete fruition.

  

Feels like a download bar on your computer being stuck in the 98%-99% range for far longer than it took to get there from 0% in the first place.

  

There is essentially no good way of describing what I mean directly other than saying, I am feeling some kind of ahedonia and it may or may not be part of just random biological fluctuations in my mind. Perhaps they were triggered by some thought, as they often are, some thought that maybe I’ve missed out on things due to these feelings and how I have to constantly reflect on these feelings to feel sane.

  

But then it becomes a self perpetuation thing. That is, my previous negative feelings are sources of my present negative feelings. It might seem like, if that is indeed the case, that I can simply put a brick down in front of the sad wheel to get it to stop, but of course that doesn’t work. If something like that worked as simple as analogy would make it seem, I feel like therapies would be one-and-done.

  

My question is how much, the extent to which I can, influence it in this way. Maybe I can supercede and obstruct the flow by not ruminating or trying to sublimate or by achieving a higher state of mindfulness. Objectifying my emotion by writing it in this vein.

  

Work and work, 28, feel and feel, experience and experience, will be 29 and surely nothing will change. From what I observe, people get better at coping but that’s all it is. If you haven’t found those that can validate and help fill the holes of questions and inexperience of your feelings, it is difficult to see it through the other side. If I had been born before the time of the internet, perhaps I would have those friends. As of now, the exist perpetually far from what I view. Anyone I do meet who I can talk to fades away. It’s inevitable as I finish this year my friends will go. Friends of convenience can never be friends for life. I am somewhat jealous of people who have lived in the same place since they were kids. Whatever friendships they’ve made surely woul dbe more enduring as they all live in the same place and shar ethe same experiences over time.

  

Experiences, we grow connection throug experiences, yet experiences are fleeting. How many experiences can you gain in 1, 2, 3 years before you depart and must start the new and somehow permanent thread of experiences to help ground your thoughts and sentiments. 

  

Hmmm. Effort and luck is always as it is. 

  

Effort and luck to maybe not feel the same through projection onto people and receive said projection back.

  

Hard to say. Works for some, doesn’t work for others.

  

Friend and partner shortcuts don’t exist no matter how much we might wish so. 

  

28 and seeking, will be 29 and having to start a new, lack of experiences due to having had no experiences. No memories other than sadness or things that have distracted it. Connection comes from anxiety of having lost the connection. Not in an anxious attachment way, but in a way that makes me question the extent to which I even want connections. To be connected or to feel connected? Difference is subtle but critical.

  

Staying up and never moving due to lack of desire to sleep because every night I sleep the closer I am to death and not really having any more chance to figure things out. Wish I figured things out.

  

The stupid thing is that, interiorly, I can see my own motivation to be like “yeah, I can’t wait for the next day to learn and get more experiences and skills ot grip the world with” but it doesn’t change anything. An example of how mind does not over come mood when we must act and feel practically. Not sure. One could inject me with cocaine and I woul probably feel hyper but the same. Not sure, wouldn’t try it though.

  

I know ther’es plenty of people like me out there, but they all exist as fantasies to me. Marketed to me in a way from profiles online, not from who they truly are. It seems like radical honesty is hard to come by. Honesty comes from expressing thoughts and emotions without reserve. How can one do this and make them public? Oxymoron. By acting in profile to others, you lose a sense of authenticity.

  

To be authentic would require no influence to be authentic.

  

Express yourself and be reminded you were told to do so. Express their expression of their desire for your expression.

  

Authenticity cannot exist, only profiles of ourselves. What we can control is the level of which we can manage our profiles. From curate to fake “raw”. From over sharing to completely withdrawn and non existent. A “private” profile is itself a profile. You cannot ignore playing the game of profiles as profiles are what we are in life too. If you meet someone not engaging in profiles, they are simply curating their own profile. 

  

I acknowledge I wish to curate my profile. I do not wish to be forced into culturally expected profiles. Shit like LinkedIn makes my skin crawl. Forcing a profile onto me to try to be accepted into a cultural expectation that has made me nothing but self doubting. Yet choosing not to is itself a profile as I’ve said. People question it, even if that seems stupid. That profile is so expected, people question it, and it’s hard to give a good reason since there is no good reason other than esoteric reasons not to engage in an aspect of culture you simply deem “to drab”. Or you blame linkedIn lunatics and overgeneralize as a coping method to just sweep the inexplicability. 

  

Profiles, curating my own, but for what purpose. To control my identity, to control my ability to be influenced or to control my ability to influence. Hard to say. We all want influence or else I’d never do this. But wanting influence is not bad. I am not so convinced one must become an ascetic to escape all inauthenticities. 

  

Even aecethtics simply seek to influence their own wold onto something. By life-denying they are life affirming, or something Nietzsche said. It is impossible to avoid profiling or desiring influence as that will have an inadvertent influence to anyone around you. You can try to be radically isolationist, which sometimes i feel heavily heavily compelled to do so.

  

Withdrawal and stop talking to everyone to somehow feel as if I have control overmyself. It is strange. Maybe then I am authentic, but I’m only being influenced to do so due the presence of other people. So it is unauthentic. I was influenced by others to do so.

  

There is no authenticity. I don’t even think it is dead, it just never existed, or at leas tin the way we imagine it today. 

  

Avoiding doing work now. Feelings have passed. Successful attempt at mindfulness I suppose, until it inevitably comes back. Daily process and effort. Leading somewhere? Seems unlikely 

  

One must not think too much of this piece. It was only ana attempt to stifle off emotion at the rawest. Whatever profile it attaches to for myself, I will have to contemplate later. Need to get back tow work.
